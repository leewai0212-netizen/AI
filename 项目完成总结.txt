───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
💡 使用示例
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

示例1 - 最简单的使用:

    from douyin_comment_scraper import DouyinCommentScraper
    
    scraper = DouyinCommentScraper(headless=False)
    try:
        comments = scraper.scrape_comments(
            video_url="https://www.douyin.com/video/123456789",
            max_comments=100,
            scroll_times=10
        )
        scraper.save_to_json()
        scraper.save_to_csv()
    finally:
        scraper.close()

示例2 - 批量爬取:

    video_urls = ["链接1", "链接2", "链接3"]
    scraper = DouyinCommentScraper()
    try:
        for idx, url in enumerate(video_urls, 1):
            comments = scraper.scrape_comments(url)
            scraper.save_to_json(f'video_{idx}.json')
    finally:
        scraper.close()

更多示例请查看 example.py 文件。
